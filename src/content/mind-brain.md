# The Mind & Brain

### **Neuroscience for a deeper understanding of how you think, feel and learn.**

The brain is a living, adaptable system shaped by biology, experience, and environment. In this pathway, we explore how brain systems contribute to attention, memory, emotion, motivation, and learning—and how researchers study these processes.

We translate established neuroscience into clear, practical ideas. You’ll build a stronger vocabulary for understanding your own experience, learn to distinguish evidence from speculation, and connect what you learn to everyday choices and habits.

## What you’ll explore

- How attention and memory support learning
- How emotion and stress can influence thought and behavior
- How experience and repetition relate to neuroplasticity
- How to approach claims about the brain with curiosity and critical thinking

**A foundation for understanding your mind—and asking better questions about how it works.**
