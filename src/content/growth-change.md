# Growth & Change

### **Psychology and habit science for lasting, meaningful transformation.**

Change is shaped by more than intention. Our routines, beliefs, relationships, and surroundings all influence the way we act and the goals we pursue. This pathway explores how these factors interact—and how to work with them more deliberately.

Drawing on psychology and habit science, we’ll examine how change unfolds over time, why setbacks happen, and how small adjustments can support progress. The emphasis is on building practices that fit your life and values, rather than chasing a perfect plan.

## What you’ll explore

- How habits form and how they can be reshaped
- The role of motivation, identity, and personal meaning
- How environment and routine influence behavior
- Ways to reflect on setbacks and adjust your approach

**Make room for steady progress and changes that matter to you.**
